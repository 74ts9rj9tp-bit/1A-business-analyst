1A Business Analyst

You make the business decisions. The analyst helps test them.

1. Submit your business plan

Paste the proposal you want to analyze. The analyst checks the plan against the assumptions needed for a baseline. Values recognized in the plan are prefilled for your confirmation; questions adapt to the business model, and each missing material input explains why it matters.

Your original plan is preserved as submitted. Financial values used by the model are confirmed separately in the interview.

2. Analyst interview

Questions are selected from gaps in your proposal. For each material gap, the analyst explains why the information matters. Mark research-needed items when you do not yet have a defensible answer.

3. Apply instructor rules

Load the published instructor configuration for your course. This application reads the rule set; it does not let students change its parameters. No class rules or rates are built into this file.

A valid instructor configuration is required before calculating the baseline.
Required configuration fields and conventions

Configuration must include schemaVersion, ruleSetId, version, forecastHorizonYears, initialOperatingCashRule.rate and basis, marketBondRateAnnual, shortTermOperatingBorrowingRateAnnual, equity.investorCount, equity.maxTotalInitialEquity, equity.maxTotalAdditionalEquity (required only if additionalEquityAllowed is true), equity.maxPerInvestor, equity.equalContributions, equity.additionalEquityAllowed, financing.shortTermBorrowingAllowed, financing.longTermBorrowingAllowed, and financing.fundingGapPolicy set to student_decides. Rates are decimals (0.08 means 8%). modelConventions must include daysPerYear, weeksPerYear, depreciationConvention set to full_year_when_in_service_at_year_start, and lossTaxTreatment set to no_tax_benefit_no_loss_carryforward.

5. Propose a decision

A proposal is tested against the currently approved version. Nothing changes until you accept it. The analyst never fills a funding gap for you.

Business Development History

Version 1 records the initial baseline. Each accepted decision creates a new immutable model version. Rejected proposals remain in the history without changing approved results.
